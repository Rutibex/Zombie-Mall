# Credits

**Zombie Mall** — created by **Michael Robinson** (2026).

The game builds on Michael Robinson's original **Ashen Covenant: The Ember Heart** engine and artwork. Some original sample resources were adapted from his *Malidor Witches Academy* work. These works are separate projects. Much of the original art (including portraits/sprite atlases) uses image generation, and original environment illustrations are generated programmatically. Music is synthesized original material.
