# Changelog

Version names below refer to the original project milestones. This file is a player-facing summary rather than an internal verification log.

## 0.33.2 — Lower-mall environmental detail (current)

- Individual retail environment graphics, floor treatments, decals and signs for lower-mall stores 1–18.
- Matching storefront portals and wayfinding in the connecting concourses.
- Cosmetic presentation updates; campaign schema 30 and save format v41 are unchanged. No save reset is required for this art update.

## 0.33.1 — Environment foundation

- Shared textures, lighting-adjacent decorative surfaces, furniture sprites and signage in opening shops and the refuge.
- No campaign reset needed for this cosmetic update.

## 0.32 — Mysteries and endings

- Persistent mystery/evidence interface, helicopter escape preparation, 150-day winter watch, and emergency/terminal decisions.
- Players with saves from older campaign versions should start a new campaign to access newly authored locations and ending consoles. Older saves retain their embedded campaign content.

## Earlier development

- Expansion to 170 connected areas, including lower mall, upper mall, rooftop and exterior.
- Six survivor classes, gear catalogue, crafting, relationships, quests, shelter systems, survival, firearms, tactical encounters and horde/awareness systems.
